# Kasauti — Sahi Bhav, Sahi Hisaab

Kasauti is a smart B2B platform for jewellery businesses.

## Core Capabilities

- **Real-time gold and silver rates** with a clear market-rate dashboard.
- **Automated customer alerts** for configurable price movements and campaign updates.
- **Digital scheme ledger management** to track customer schemes, installments, balances, and settlements.
- **AI-powered assistant** to answer business, product, and operational queries.
- **Intelligent alert agent** to surface actionable insights (rate spikes, due collections, and customer follow-ups).

## Intended Business Value

Kasauti helps jewellery businesses reduce manual work, improve communication speed, maintain accurate customer scheme records, and make faster data-driven decisions.
